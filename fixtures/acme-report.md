# Acme Corporation — Annual Report 2024

## Financial Summary

Acme Corporation reported total revenue of $847.3 million for fiscal year 2024,
an increase of 18.4% over the $715.6 million recorded in 2023. Gross margin
improved to 62.1% from 58.7%, driven primarily by a shift in product mix toward
higher-margin subscription offerings.

Operating income reached $112.8 million, compared to $74.2 million in the prior
year. Net income was $89.4 million, or $2.31 per diluted share.

## Revenue by Segment

The Cloud Platform segment generated $512.9 million, representing 60.5% of total
revenue and growing 27.3% year over year. This was the primary driver of overall
growth. The Hardware segment contributed $221.1 million, roughly flat against
2023's $218.4 million. Professional Services accounted for the remaining $113.3
million, up 9.1%.

## Headcount and Operations

Total headcount stood at 4,182 employees as of December 31, 2024, up from 3,617
a year earlier. Research and development expense was $167.2 million, or 19.7% of
revenue. The company opened a new engineering centre in Bangalore in September
2024, which accounted for 214 of the net new hires.

## Risks

Management identified customer concentration as a principal risk: the three
largest customers together represented 31.4% of total revenue in 2024. Supply
chain constraints in the Hardware segment are expected to persist through the
first half of 2025.

## Outlook

For fiscal 2025, management guided to revenue between $960 million and $1.01
billion, implying growth of 13.3% to 19.2%. Capital expenditure is expected to
be approximately $78 million, weighted toward data centre capacity.
