# Acme Corporation — Q1 FY2025 Quarterly Review

## Purpose of This Document

This review covers the first quarter of fiscal year 2025, ending 31 March 2025.
It is a companion to the FY2024 Annual Report and deliberately restates several
prior-year figures for comparison. Where the two documents disagree, the Annual
Report is authoritative for FY2024 and this review is authoritative for Q1
FY2025.

## Quarterly Financial Summary

Acme Corporation reported total revenue of $231.4 million for Q1 FY2025, an
increase of 21.7% over the $190.1 million recorded in Q1 FY2024. Gross margin
was 63.8%, up from 62.1% for the full FY2024 year.

Operating income for the quarter was $34.6 million, compared with $22.9 million
in the prior-year quarter. Net income was $27.1 million, or $0.70 per diluted
share.

Free cash flow was $41.2 million, exceeding net income for the third consecutive
quarter, driven by favourable deferred revenue timing on annual subscription
renewals.

## Revenue by Segment

The Cloud Platform segment generated $146.8 million in Q1 FY2025, representing
63.4% of total revenue and growing 31.2% year over year. This is a higher share
than the 60.5% Cloud Platform represented across the whole of FY2024.

The Hardware segment contributed $52.1 million, declining 4.6% year over year.
This is the second consecutive quarter of Hardware decline and management now
describes the segment as being in managed wind-down for two of its four product
lines.

Professional Services accounted for the remaining $32.5 million, up 14.2% year
over year. Services attach rate on new Cloud Platform contracts rose to 38%
from 31%.

## Geographic Breakdown

North America contributed $138.9 million, or 60.0% of revenue. EMEA contributed
$62.5 million, or 27.0%. Asia-Pacific contributed $30.0 million, or 13.0%.

EMEA was the fastest-growing region at 34.1% year over year, driven primarily by
three large public-sector contracts in Germany and the Netherlands. Asia-Pacific
grew 11.4%, below plan, which management attributed to a delayed product launch
in Japan.

## Headcount and Operations

Total headcount stood at 4,510 employees as of 31 March 2025, up from 4,182 at
the end of FY2024. Net hiring was concentrated in Cloud Platform engineering,
which added 214 of the 328 net new roles.

Research and development expense was $44.1 million for the quarter, or 19.1% of
revenue, slightly below the 19.7% recorded for FY2024. The Bangalore engineering
centre reached 340 staff, ahead of its 300-person target for the quarter.

Voluntary attrition was 9.2% on an annualised basis, down from 12.8% in FY2024.

## Customer Concentration

The three largest customers together represented 28.9% of total revenue in Q1
FY2025, an improvement from 31.4% in FY2024 but still above the 25% ceiling the
board has set as a target.

The single largest customer, an unnamed North American logistics operator,
accounted for 12.1% of quarterly revenue. Its contract renews in Q3 FY2026 and
is identified as the single largest revenue risk in the register.

Net revenue retention was 114%, and gross logo churn was 4.1% annualised.

## Risks

Management identified four principal risks for the coming quarters.

First, customer concentration remains above target, as described above.

Second, supply chain constraints in the Hardware segment are expected to persist
through the first half of calendar 2026, six months longer than the previous
guidance given in the FY2024 Annual Report.

Third, the Japan product launch delay creates a risk to the Asia-Pacific full
year plan. The launch has slipped from Q2 to Q4 FY2025.

Fourth, and new this quarter, competitive pricing pressure in the mid-market
Cloud Platform tier reduced average selling price by 3.4% sequentially.

## Outlook

Management guides to full-year FY2025 revenue of $980 million to $1.01 billion,
implying growth of 15.7% to 19.2% over FY2024. Gross margin is expected to
remain between 63% and 64%.

The Hardware segment is expected to decline between 3% and 6% for the full year.
Cloud Platform is expected to grow between 26% and 30%.

Capital expenditure guidance is unchanged at $58 million for the year.

## Board and Governance Changes

Two new independent directors were appointed effective 1 February 2025. The
audit committee was expanded from three to four members. No changes were made to
executive compensation arrangements during the quarter.
